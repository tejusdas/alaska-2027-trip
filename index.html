Current Recommendation

⚓ Royal Princess — 7 nights, roundtrip Seattle

Cheapest for the group overall once flights are included — despite HAL's lower cruise fare, Dallas→Vancouver flights cost enough more that Royal Princess wins on total cost by roughly $1,000. Confirmed Glacier Bay day, downtown Ketchikan port, balanced for a mixed-age group (kids ages 6–14). It's the value pick, not necessarily the fun-factor or glacier-views pick — see the scorecard above if those matter more to your family.

~$16,200–19,800 total, all 10 travelers Saturday departures Downtown Ketchikan
Decisions Still Needed From the Group
  • Which ship? Royal Princess (cheapest overall) vs. HAL Koningsdam (better itinerary — two glacier days, no Victoria — for ~$1,000 more) vs. Norwegian Encore (best for teens specifically, but $5,000+ more).
  • Which exact date? Royal Princess: Jul 17, 24, 31, or Aug 7, 2027.
  • Cabin layout: 3 balconies — Rakesh's family of 4 needs the largest cabin; adjacent/connecting cabins preferred but not required.

What's Been Ruled Out, and Why

  • Royal Caribbean — no 2027 Seattle sailing includes Glacier Bay.
  • Star Princess / Norwegian Bliss — sister ships to our shortlist, but their 2027 sailings get Endicott Arm & Dawes Glacier instead of Glacier Bay.
  • Emerald Princess — Glacier Bay isn't guaranteed on every sailing (varies by date), and it's an older ship than the rest of the Princess fleet considered.
  • HAL Zaandam — Koningsdam's smaller, older sister ship, with the weakest kids' club of any option considered.
  • Land tour / Denali add-on — not pursued; trip is cruise-only by request.
